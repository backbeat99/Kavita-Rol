# RPG catalog

The RPG catalog distinguishes a game, its publications and the readable versions of each publication.

## Language

**Game**:
The RPG line under which publications and other materials are organized. Its credits are not inherited from an individual publication.

**Publication**:
A bibliographic RPG work with its own title, authors and designers; it may have multiple readable versions.

**Version**:
A readable copy of a publication, with its own file and language. Publication credits do not become version credits.

**Resource**:
A standalone practical item associated with a game, such as a map, character sheet or play aid. It is not a publication version.

**Game tag**:
A descriptive label for a game as a whole. It is independent from tags assigned to versions or resources.

**Material tag**:
A descriptive label for one version or one resource. It does not become a game tag and is not assigned to a publication as a whole.
_Avoid_: File tag

**Publication writer**:
A person credited as a writer or designer of a publication, regardless of how many versions that publication has. This is a Writer credit in People, not a game-wide or version credit.
